# Conditional stock recommendations

The software dashboard links to the recommendation panel on `decision.html`.
This is an explicitly hypothetical quality-growth screen, not a validated return
prediction, intrinsic-value model, personalized allocation or automated trade.

## Inputs and calculations

Financial inputs come from the existing FMP endpoint. Recommendations require
SEC-reconciled TTM revenue, operating income, operating cash flow, capital
expenditure and stock compensation, with filing links. Existing freshness,
currency, history and unresolved-data gates remain mandatory. Quotes and market
capitalization come from Yahoo. No missing value is filled with invented data.

Illustrative bear/base/bull P/S assumptions are 6/8/10; adjusted-cash yields are
4%/3%/2%. These are editable strategy assumptions, not observed peer multiples,
analyst consensus or company-specific estimates. They reset on reload.

- Adjusted cash = TTM FCF minus 100% of SBC, a conservative dilution-cost proxy.
- Scenario equity value = min(TTM revenue × P/S, adjusted cash ÷ cash yield).
- Scenario price = quote × scenario equity value ÷ quoted market capitalization.
- Entry ceiling = min(base price × (1 − discount), existing P/S and FCF-yield
  screen price ceilings). Default discount is 20%.

Financials, share count and FX are constant across scenarios. There is no forecast
growth or enterprise-value/debt bridge. Debt and liquidity need a human review.
Any scenarios displayed for unreconciled inputs are provisional; their entry
ceiling is withheld. Invalid inputs clear scenario outputs.

## Labels

- **Insufficient evidence**: missing, stale, incompatible or unreconciled inputs.
- **Avoid for now**: excluded by selected layers, growth/FCF margin, positive
  operating income, positive adjusted cash or position/drawdown gates. Not a
  prediction of falling prices.
- **Wait for a better price**: quality/risk gates pass but price exceeds ceiling.
- **Review thesis first**: price also passes, but qualitative review is incomplete.
- **Buy candidate**: all above gates plus a current user-attested thesis review.

Review requires an HTTPS source link and notes on thesis, value-chain advantage,
balance sheet, catalyst, risks and invalidation. It is a completion check, not
independent verification of the link or the user's statements. Editing notes or
changing the financial period invalidates the review. Only explicitly saved notes
persist in browser local storage. No source or catalyst is generated by this panel.

Value-chain fit combines the existing selected taxonomy layers with the user's
documented competitive-advantage review. It does not infer or verify economic
relationships merely from a company's position on the map.
