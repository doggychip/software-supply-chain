How to read these recommendations

These are dated assistant judgments based on business quality, valuation, value-chain position and risks, not outputs of the automated screen. Confidence is moderate unless a card says otherwise; returns are not guaranteed.

Prices come from FMP and may be delayed; quotes are cached for five minutes. There is no Yahoo price fallback. Missing or invalid prices remain unavailable. A new price does not revalue a thesis. Material price moves or company news can invalidate an opinion before its next review date.

Opinions move to “Needs a fresh review” at the stated review date, or at most seven days after review. This is an editorial safeguard, not a forecast or an automatic research refresh. There is no live news monitoring on this page.

Sell/reduce does not account for your tax lots, portfolio concentration or cash needs. No trades are placed. Notes you enter on the advanced page stay separate from these opinions.
