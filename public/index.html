Source hierarchy and limits

  • Primary: standardized US-GAAP or IFRS facts filed by each issuer with SEC EDGAR. Every displayed fact includes its concept, period, unit and filing link.
  • SEC does not guarantee the scope or accuracy of its ticker-to-CIK association file, so the mapped SEC entity name and CIK are shown for inspection.
  • Reconciliation: Yahoo Finance public endpoints. Yahoo values never replace a missing issuer-filed value.
  • “Aligned” means the period ends match and the numeric difference is at most 0.5%; it does not prove identical accounting bases. Yahoo EPS may be adjusted while SEC diluted EPS is filed GAAP/IFRS.
  • Market prices have no comparable issuer filing value, so Yahoo quotes are shown only as secondary market context.
  • The layer map is a curated taxonomy dated —, not an issuer fact, assessment score or recommendation.
