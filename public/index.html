Data provenance and limits

  • Prices, history, options, earnings fields and analyst estimates come from unofficial Yahoo Finance public endpoints through the server proxy.
  • The layer map is a curated coverage taxonomy dated —; it is not a market fact or recommendation.
  • Analyst targets, consensus and forward growth are estimates—not verified company results.
  • When a live field is absent or stale, the dashboard shows “—” or excludes the record. It never substitutes a stored number.
  • Verify decision-critical values with the relevant exchange, SEC filing or issuer release.
