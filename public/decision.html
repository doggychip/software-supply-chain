Source and interpretation boundaries

  • Primary: reported financial facts and filing links from SEC EDGAR company facts.
  • Reconciliation: current price, market capitalization and daily history from unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoints.
  • Value-chain fit: the curated map supplies company classifications; selected layers are your strategy preference, not a factual claim of layer quality or importance.
  • Revenue growth uses a comparable prior-year quarter when available, otherwise comparable annual periods.
  • Issuer guidance is not standardized and is never invented. The journal requires you to preserve the official source yourself.
  • “Research now” means only that the available evidence passes your inputs; it is not a prediction, price target or instruction to trade.
